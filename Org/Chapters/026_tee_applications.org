*** Remote Attestation in TEEs
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: remote_attestation
:END:

*Remote attestation* enables one party, the *verifier*, to cryptographically verify the hardware platform and software configuration of a remote party, the *attester*. The main objective is for the *verifier* to gather sufficient information about the attester, typically provided by the attester, and to integrate measurements and cryptographic proofs. Enabling the verifier to establish a trusted connection with a peer or service. In mutual attestation, both peers can attest to themselves to ensure trust in both, thereby guaranteeing integrity and authenticity in their trusted execution environments.
