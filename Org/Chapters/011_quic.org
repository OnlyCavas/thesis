** QUIC: A Modern Transport Protocol for Low-Latency, Secure Communication
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: quic_intro
:END:

BitTorrent thrives on raw, collective bandwidth as peers exchange multiple chunks in parallel, while choking freeloaders and prompting reciprocity. However, it inherits problems from its underlying transport protocol (TCP/HTTP/1.1) regarding congestion awareness. TCP's slow start mechanism and congestion control features underperform on short-lived, bursty connections typical of P2P transfers. Additionally, if a single packet is lost, the entire stream stalls due to head-of-line blocking, reducing overall throughput. The following sections examine whether QUIC would be better suited for P2P transfers, address the head-of-line blocking issue, and provide multiplexed streams within a single P2P connection.
